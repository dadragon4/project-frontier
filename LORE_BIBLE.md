# LORE_BIBLE.md — PROJECT FRONTIER

Everything here is the user's lore. Nothing in this file has been invented by the agent. Status labels are defined in `AGENTS.md`; register entries (`Cn`, `Qn`) are in `DECISIONS.md`.

Items marked **(ruling 2026-11-10)** are the user's direct decisions settling earlier conflicts between the concept doc and the master prompt; they override both.

Spellings are kept exactly as the user wrote them ("Spacial", "Frontier Corp").

## The universe [Established]

- The universe has no finalized name and does not need one. Planets, colonies, orbital installations and regions exist within it.
- Humanity has spread far beyond Earth across distant planetary systems and orbital environments.
- Technology and biological adaptation have changed how people live. Those who can afford it move between worlds toward ever more advanced lifestyles.
- As civilization moves on, remote colonies are abandoned along with their infrastructure, technology, resources and communities. Those who stay maintain what the expanding civilization no longer considers worth maintaining.
- The resulting divide:
  - those who advance with the SGA,
  - those abandoned by the SGA,
  - those who profit from the remnants of the old world.

## Organizations

### Spacial Alliance for Freedom (SAF) [Established]

The original organization and historical predecessor of the SGA. It represents an earlier political identity and period.

### Spacial Government of Advancement (SGA) [Established]

- The dominant governing authority that succeeded the SAF. It believes continued technological, scientific and biological advancement under centralized authority is necessary to preserve humanity's future.
- That pursuit has left whole populations and colonies behind. It is not simply evil; its methods are increasingly questioned.
- It refuses to recognize Aero's sovereignty.
- **(ruling 2026-11-10)** The SGA believes it has the right to set galactic law because it holds the longest-running line of humans in power, dating back to the era of technology — the age of the 2000s.
- **(ruling 2026-11-10)** The SGA has a normal military in addition to Frontier.

### Frontier — the project and the Corp [Established — ruling 2026-11-10, settles C1 and C2]

- **Frontier** is first the name of the SGA's research and development project, which includes the bio-enhancement work.
- The name is later also given to the **Frontier Corp**: the special corp whose soldiers receive those bio-enhancements. It is one corp — a special force — of the SGA, distinct from the SGA's normal military.
- The Frontier Corp followed the aftermath of the civilian shuttle attack, enforcing SGA galactic laws.
- It is the force that goes back to the remote colonies to gather resources and take back control.
- From the concept doc, still standing: the Frontier project reached a technological breakpoint requiring resources previously deemed unnecessary, which is what sends the SGA back to the colonies it abandoned.
- The Frontier Corp is one of the two starting playable affiliations.

This replaces the master prompt's description of the Corp as "the consolidation of multiple armies" and its statement that the enhancement initiative had no name.

### Federation of Aero (also written "Aero Federation") [Established]

- Formed hundreds of years after the shuttle attack **(ruling 2026-11-10)**.
- Founded by **Lorenz Aero** and a small group of friends who began by maintaining the systems and infrastructure humanity's expansion left behind.
- Grew into a contract-for-hire organization — maintenance, protection, resource recovery, contracted services — and then, through reputation and accumulated resources, into a federation operating across the darker sectors of former SAF/SGA colonization. It is a significant political and military power.
- Lorenz rules with an iron hand but shows real compassion and personal commitment to those around him. Friends and family hold positions in his cabinet; Aero's identity is family-centred. His original vision: protect neglected populations, provide services, stay independent of centralized authority.

**The fall of Lorenz [Established — ruling 2026-11-10, settles C3]**

- Blame for the civilian shuttle attack shifted from the SGA to the Federation of Aero. People began to question Lorenz's authority.
- Lorenz's **brother**, corrupted by power and greed, overthrew him and installed his own son as leader.
- That son — **Lorenz's nephew, not yet named** — is the corrupt successor. He rules differently from Lorenz, corrupting the cabinet and turning the federation toward galactic influence and control.
- Lorenz went into hiding, revealing that he had a child: **Enzo Hart**.
- Aero is therefore not automatically the good alternative to the SGA.

### SEVs and Aero's contractors [Established — ruling 2026-11-10, settles C11]

- **SEVs** — *Scavenge. Evolve. Vindicate.* — are officially employed by Aero. They have more advanced gear and bio-enhancements. They are the second starting playable affiliation, recovering technology and resources toward Aero's objectives.
- **Independent contractors** hired by Aero have less advanced gear but higher stamina and movement speed.
- These differences may become gameplay mechanics; that is to be discussed later (**[Open]**).

### Enzo Hart and the Children of Aero [Established — ruling 2026-11-10, settles C3 and C4]

- Enzo Hart is **Lorenz Aero's child**, not his nephew. (This replaces the master prompt's "nephew of Lorenz".)
- He grew up in secrecy and exile with his father. He grew in the arts of speech, cultural history, technological fluency, and above all a love for doing good.
- Wanting to establish peace within the galaxy, he founded the **Children of Aero**, aiming to gain control over what was originally his father's, and to bring good, peace and trust to the human race.
- From the earlier sources, still standing: the movement seeks to restore the federation to Lorenz's original ideals and free remote colonies from SGA authority; it opposes both SGA centralization and Aero's corrupted leadership.
- They are a hidden movement. They are never shown outright. Players meet references to them — documents, artifacts, intelligence files, voice logs, environmental storytelling, hidden locations, unusual encounters — long before understanding who they are. They later become playable through an unlock (see `GAMEPLAY.md`).

### Three ideological directions [Established]

- **SGA:** advancement, consolidation, control.
- **Federation of Aero:** power and galactic dominance under corrupted leadership.
- **Children of Aero:** restoration, independence, Lorenz's original ideals.

None is entirely good or evil.

## The civilian shuttle attack [Established]

A devastating terrorist attack on a civilian shuttle is a turning point in history. It is a **false-flag operation**. The perpetrator is unknown in the present day, to the public and to the player. Both the SGA and the Federation of Aero use propaganda to shift blame; blame moved from the SGA onto the Federation of Aero, which undermined Lorenz. **The perpetrator is intentionally undecided** and this thread must not be casually resolved.

## Biological adaptations [Established]

- Uncommon among ordinary people.
- More common among soldiers, who must withstand the physical demands of moving between planets and environments. The SGA's Frontier project produces such soldiers for the Frontier Corp.
- SEVs also have bio-enhancements **(ruling 2026-11-10)**.
- Also relatively common among the Children of Aero, because of constant movement, covert activity and undercover work.
- Enhanced does not automatically mean superhuman. Gameplay expression is **[Open]**.

## The religious and cosmic mystery [Established]

- **The one true God blessed Earth.** Within the setting this is an established fact, a cultural belief, and central to the Children of Aero's perspective.
- Many deny it or believe it never happened, influenced by propaganda and competing narratives. Some groups suppress it.
- Science has never fully explained the universe's history.
- Other planets hold unusual life and resources of unknown origin: rare minerals, unusual food ingredients, resources with exceptional technological properties, dark matter used in advanced technology, and alien life that exists but is uncommon.
- These are scarce and often held in strongholds, abandoned facilities and contested installations. Scarcity drives exploration, raids and military conflict.
- The mystery is preserved. No definitive explanation may be written without the user's approval.

## Historical timeline [Established, incorporating the 2026-11-10 rulings]

1. **The age of the 2000s** — the era of technology, to which the SGA traces its line of power.
2. **The Spacial Alliance for Freedom (SAF)** — the original organization.
3. **The shuttle attack** — false flag, perpetrator unknown.
4. **The Frontier Corp and the rise of the SGA** — the Corp follows the aftermath of the attack, enforcing SGA galactic law; the SGA becomes the dominant authority, advancing while leaving colonies behind.
5. **Hundreds of years later: the formation of Aero** — Lorenz and friends, contractors growing into a federation.
6. **The fall of Lorenz** — blame for the attack shifts onto Aero; Lorenz's brother overthrows him and installs his son.
7. **The corruption of Aero** — the nephew turns the federation toward galactic control.
8. **The Children of Aero** — Enzo Hart, raised in exile by Lorenz, founds the hidden movement.

**[Open] [C13]** Two ordering points to confirm:

- The attack is placed hundreds of years before Aero exists, yet blame for it later shifts onto Aero and brings down Lorenz. Is that intended — a centuries-old atrocity pinned on Aero through propaganda — or is the attack closer in time to Aero?
- Frontier is the R&D project's name first and the Corp's name later, and the Corp follows the attack. So did the Frontier project already exist before the attack, and is the push to reclaim the colonies (after the project's "breakpoint") a present-day development?

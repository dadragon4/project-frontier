# LORE_BIBLE.md — PROJECT FRONTIER

Everything here is the user's lore. Where the concept doc and the master prompt differ, **both versions are recorded** and the conflict is tracked in `TASKS.md`. Nothing in this file has been invented by the agent. Status labels are defined in `AGENTS.md`.

Spellings are kept exactly as the user wrote them ("Spacial", "Frontier Corp").

## The universe [Established]

- The universe has no finalized name and does not need one. Planets, colonies, orbital installations and regions exist within it.
- Humanity has spread far beyond Earth across distant planetary systems and orbital environments.
- Technology and biological adaptation have changed how people live. Those who can afford it move between worlds toward ever more advanced lifestyles.
- As civilization moves on, remote colonies are abandoned along with their infrastructure, technology, resources and communities. Those who stay maintain what the expanding civilization no longer considers worth maintaining.
- The resulting divide:
  - those who advance with the SGA,
  - those abandoned by the SGA,
  - those who profit from the remnants of the old world.

## Organizations

### Spacial Alliance for Freedom (SAF) [Established]

The original organization and historical predecessor of the SGA. It represents an earlier political identity and period.

### Spacial Government of Advancement (SGA) [Established]

The dominant governing authority that succeeded the SAF. It believes continued technological, scientific and biological advancement under centralized authority is necessary to preserve humanity's future. That pursuit has left whole populations and colonies behind. It is not simply evil; its methods are increasingly questioned. It refuses to recognize Aero's sovereignty.

### Frontier Corp — and what "Frontier" names [Conflict C1, C2]

Agreed in both sources: the Frontier Corp is the SGA's military arm and one of the two starting playable affiliations. It is deployed to reclaim territories and resources the SGA previously abandoned.

Where the sources differ:

| | Concept doc | Master prompt |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |

The SGA redirects its achievements to military use and deploys bio-enhanced soldiers to reclaim remote colonies. "The Frontier Corp becomes its boots-on-the-ground task force." | Frontier Corp is the **consolidation of multiple armies** into one force, **separate from** the SGA's experimental R&D. Do not treat them as interchangeable. The enhancement initiative's official name **is not yet established**. |

Story section: it emerges when the Frontier project is turned to military use. | After consolidation of armies (consistent with the timeline entry). |

Agreed regardless: the SGA runs experimental R&D including biological enhancement; selected soldiers are enhanced and sent to remote colonies and demanding environments.

### Federation of Aero (also written "Aero Federation") [Established]

- Founded by **Lorenz Aero** and a small group of friends who began by maintaining the systems and infrastructure humanity's expansion left behind.
- Grew into a contract-for-hire organization — maintenance, protection, resource recovery, contracted services — and then, through reputation and accumulated resources, into a federation operating across the darker sectors of former SAF/SGA colonization. It is a significant political and military power.
- Lorenz rules with an iron hand but shows real compassion and personal commitment to those around him. Friends and family hold positions in his cabinet; Aero's identity is family-centred. His original vision: protect neglected populations, provide services, stay independent of centralized authority.
- **Corruption:** a later successor takes control, corrupts the cabinet and turns the federation toward galactic influence and control. Aero is therefore not automatically the good alternative to the SGA.
- Who that successor is: **[Conflict C3]**.

### SEVs [Established]

Aero's operators and the second starting playable affiliation. *Scavenge. Evolve. Vindicate.* They recover technology and resources while working toward Aero's objectives.

Employment status differs slightly between sources (**[Conflict C11]**): the concept doc calls them "independent scavengers and contractors"; the master prompt says they are officially employed by Aero, though some operate as independent contractors under its authority.

### Enzo Hart and the Children of Aero

**[Established]**

- Enzo Hart founds the Children of Aero (as a young adult, per the master prompt).
- Goal: restore the federation to Lorenz's original ideals and free remote colonies from SGA authority. They oppose both SGA centralization and Aero's corrupted leadership.
- They are a hidden movement. Players meet references to them — documents, artifacts, intelligence files, environmental storytelling, hidden locations, unusual encounters, references in conversation — long before understanding who they are.
- They are not selectable at the start. How they become playable: see `GAMEPLAY.md` (**[Conflict C5]**).

### Three ideological directions [Established]

- **SGA:** advancement, consolidation, control.
- **Federation of Aero:** power and galactic dominance under corrupted leadership.
- **Children of Aero:** restoration, independence, Lorenz's original ideals.

None is entirely good or evil.

## The civilian shuttle attack [Established]

A devastating terrorist attack on a civilian shuttle is a turning point in history. It is a **false-flag operation**. The perpetrator is unknown in the present day, to the public and to the player. Both the SGA and the Federation of Aero use propaganda to shift blame. **The perpetrator is intentionally undecided** and this thread must not be casually resolved.

## Biological adaptations [Established]

- Uncommon among ordinary people.
- More common among soldiers, who must withstand the physical demands of moving between planets and environments. The SGA invests in experimental R&D to produce such soldiers.
- Also relatively common among the Children of Aero, because of constant movement, covert activity and undercover work.
- Enhanced does not automatically mean superhuman. Gameplay expression is **[Open]**.

## The religious and cosmic mystery [Established]

- **The one true God blessed Earth.** Within the setting this is an established fact, a cultural belief, and central to the Children of Aero's perspective.
- Many deny it or believe it never happened, influenced by propaganda and competing narratives. Some groups suppress it.
- Science has never fully explained the universe's history.
- Other planets hold unusual life and resources of unknown origin: rare minerals, unusual food ingredients, resources with exceptional technological properties, dark matter used in advanced technology, and alien life that exists but is uncommon.
- These are scarce and often held in strongholds, abandoned facilities and contested installations. Scarcity drives exploration, raids and military conflict.
- The mystery is preserved. No definitive explanation may be written without the user's approval.

## Historical timeline [Established, as ordered in the concept doc]

1. **The Spacial Alliance for Freedom (SAF)** — the original organization.
2. **The shuttle attack** — false flag; prompts consolidation of military authority and the establishment of the Frontier Corp (see **[Conflict C2]**).
3. **The rise of the SGA** — dominant authority; advancement that leaves colonies behind.
4. **The formation of Aero** — Lorenz and friends, contractors growing into a federation.
5. **The corruption of Aero** — the successor turns the federation toward galactic control.
6. **The Children of Aero** — Enzo Hart's hidden movement.

## Narrative delivery [Established]

The central story advances through seasonal updates. Further narrative is discovered through unmarked locations, hidden rooms and facilities, artifacts and documents, recovered intelligence, environmental storytelling, and encounters with hidden factions and unusual technology.
